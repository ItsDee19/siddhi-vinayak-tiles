Privacy & cookies

You can explore our catalogues without creating an account. Here is how this website handles your information and optional services.

Updated 19 September 2026

Enquiries you choose to share

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser. Your name, phone number and project details stay in the page’s memory until you choose Review in WhatsApp. Opening that link passes the draft to WhatsApp; you decide whether to send it there.

The website has no form submission server and does not save your form fields in browser storage. Reloading or closing the page clears the draft. Once you contact the showroom through WhatsApp or by phone, your conversation is handled through that service and by the showroom to respond to your enquiry.

A shared catalogue link includes the selected collection, page and design. It does not include your contact form details.

Cookies & browser storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses small browser storage entries for the following purposes:

  • Your cookie choice: sidhhi-cookie-preferences in local storage remembers whether you allow the optional Google Maps embed. The preference expires after 180 days, when we ask again. It contains your choice and its date, not your form details.
  • Repeated enquiry protection: sidhhi-enquiry-cooldown in session storage holds a preparation time to limit repeated drafts within 30 seconds. It contains no enquiry text and is cleared when the browser tab’s session ends.

You can change your choice through Cookie preferences, or remove these entries using your browser’s site-data controls. If preference storage is unavailable, the embedded map stays off.

Optional Google Maps

We load the embedded map only after you allow it. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which may receive your IP address and device information and may use cookies under Google’s privacy policy.

You can withdraw permission in Cookie preferences. This removes the embed and stops further embedded-map requests; it cannot delete information already received by Google or its existing cookies. The separate directions link opens Google Maps only when you choose to visit it.

Hosting & external services

Vercel hosts this website. Requests for pages and catalogue images include technical information such as your IP address, browser information and requested URL. The hosting provider may process this information to deliver and protect the service. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

Fonts and catalogue artwork are served with this website. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other external websites are governed by those services’ own policies once you open them.
